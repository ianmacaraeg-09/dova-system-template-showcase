Straight talk

What you see is a demo.

The templates run in your browser on invented data, so a refresh keeps your changes and a reset brings the samples back. Logins, roles and payments are simulated. Going live means connecting a real, secure backend. That is the part we build with you.

Questions

Before you ask.

Can I use my own branding and terms?

Yes. Names, logos, levels, rooms, rates, fees and terminology are data in each template and are replaced for your business.

Is my data safe in the demo?

The demos have no backend. Anything you type stays in your own browser. Please do not enter real personal or business data.

Can a template be changed beyond settings?

Yes. Each is a complete codebase, so custom workflows, reports and integrations can be added. We scope that with you.

What if my business is not on this list?

These are starting points. Tell us the process you want to run and we build around it.
